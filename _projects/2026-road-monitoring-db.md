Part of the National Road Monitoring System program, building the indicator
database that tracks the condition and performance of the national road
network.

**Sponsor**: 국토연구원 (KRIHS)
**Period**: 2026.07 – 2026.12

The project builds and manages base road-network data to support road
policy, analyses public statistics for the monitoring-indicator DB,
performs GIS analysis for road-policy performance assessment, and defines
the monitoring indicators and their calculation methodology.
